ABOUT BHOOMISETU

Unified National Land Information Management System & Citizen Revenue Services

General Information

Unified National Land Registry Architecture

BhoomiSetu (भूमि सेतु) serves as the singular digital gateway established under the Digital India Land Records Modernization Programme (DILRMP). It seamlessly bridges 28 States and 8 Union Territories with a unified land transaction protocol, computerized Record of Rights (RoR), and GIS cadastral maps.

CORE SERVICES & PROGRAMMES

Integrated Digital Governance Modules Facilitating Hassle-Free Citizen Land Services

What is Bhu-Aadhaar (ULPIN)?

A unique 14-digit alphanumeric geo-tagged identity assigned to every survey parcel in India based on longitude and latitude coordinates.

Know More >

Online RoR & Digital Mutation

Instant download of digitally signed Record of Rights (Khatauni/Khasra/Patta) with QR code verification and seamless mutation tracking.

Know More >

Geo-Referenced Cadastral Maps

High-resolution satellite imagery overlayed with survey parcel boundaries via BhuNaksha GIS platform for precise area demarcation.

Know More >

Revenue Court Dispute Tracker

Online repository of land dispute litigation, hearing calendar, automated stay-order checks, and judicial status updates.
