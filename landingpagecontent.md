# One important design decision

I would **not put “SIH 2026” prominently inside the actual product UI**.

The product should look like a **real government platform that could theoretically be deployed**, while your PPT/presentation explains that it is your **SIH 2026 prototype**.

Also avoid writing:

> “AI-powered everything”

Instead, use government/product terminology:

**GIS-enabled | Data-driven | Predictive Analytics | Decision Support | Secure | Transparent**

That's much more credible for this problem because the official specification itself frames the solution around **AI, GIS & Data Analytics for Public Administration and Infrastructure Management**.
